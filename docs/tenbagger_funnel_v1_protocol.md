# Tenbagger Funnel v1 — pre-registered research protocol
Status: FROZEN BEFORE HISTORICAL OUTCOME TESTING

Objective: rank stocks by empirical probability of achieving >=10x total price return within 5-10 years, while separately reporting downside/failure rates. This is not the Winner Funnel and must not alter it.

## Competing hypotheses (test, do not blend until OOS evidence)
A Growth/inflection: small size; revenue growth >=15%; acceleration; improving margins/FCF; low leverage/dilution; positive but not necessarily extreme momentum.
B Value/recovery: small size; low P/S, P/B or high FCF yield; improving operations; price below prior high / weak-to-neutral momentum; balance-sheet survival.

## Historical labels
For every eligible stock/date calculate forward max total return and terminal return at 3y, 5y and 10y where history permits.
Labels: 2x, 3x, 5x, 10x. Also failure labels: -50%, bankruptcy/delisting where data permits.

## PIT
Fundamentals only after filed/public date. Prices only through ranking date. Entry next tradable session.

## Universe
Historical investable US universe first. Include delisted/failed securities where source permits. Current-survivor-only result = diagnostic, never validation.
Sweden held out until credible historical PIT fundamentals/universe are available.

## Metrics
base rate and precision/lift for 2x/3x/5x/10x in top 1%, 5%, 10%; recall; median forward return; downside rate; calibration; sector/size/year cohorts; winner-exclusion; transaction-cost sensitivity for portfolio simulation.

## Anti-overfit
No final-period tuning. Development/training and validation periods separated chronologically. Candidate production uses only a specification frozen before final OOS test.
